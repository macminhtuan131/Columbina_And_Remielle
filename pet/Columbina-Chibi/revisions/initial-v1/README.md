ornaments, crossed ribbon ties, plum tassels, and blue-white outfit. Gemielle's
contact sheet defines the seated clipboard pose and animation gesture family.

and Pets preflight all passed. `qa/` preserves their reports. Automated continuity
warnings around feather silhouettes and loop boundaries were visually reviewed;
the feather gaps are open background, and no body holes or direction reversals
were observed. `decoded/`, `references/`, and `prompts/` preserve the source work.
